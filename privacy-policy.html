This privacy policy explains how Bodymodo ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application.

Bodymodo is operated by [YOUR FULL NAME] as an independent developer. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at abdeyamine@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

1.1 Account information (when you sign in)

When you create an account or sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A unique user ID generated by Firebase Authentication

We use this only to authenticate you and let you sign back in on other devices.

1.2 Fitness and health data (stored on your device)

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Profile: age, gender, height, weight, goal weight, fitness goal, training environment (home or gym), fitness level, weekly workout frequency
  • Workout history: dates, durations, difficulty ratings, energy levels, exercises completed
  • Body measurements: weight log over time
  • Nutrition: meals you log, water intake
  • Active program and challenge progress

This data lives in a private database on your device. If you uninstall Bodymodo, it is deleted.

1.3 Diagnostic information

To fix bugs and improve stability, we collect anonymous crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics. This includes:

  • Crash stack traces
  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Non-personal log messages (warnings and errors)

Crash reports do not include your name, email, fitness data, or any other personal information.

1.4 Notifications

If you enable workout reminders, the App schedules them locally on your device using Android's WorkManager. Reminder settings are stored on your device only. No notification data is sent to our servers.

2. What we DO NOT collect

For clarity, Bodymodo does not collect or use:

  • Location data (GPS or otherwise)
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts, calendar, or photos
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics about how you use the app
  • Payment information (the app does not currently support in-app purchases)
  • Data from any other apps on your device

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

4. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Let you sign in and access your account on multiple devices
  • Show you your workout history, programs, and progress
  • Send you the workout reminders you've explicitly enabled
  • Diagnose and fix crashes

We do not use your information to:

  • Send marketing emails
  • Build advertising profiles
  • Train AI models
  • Share with data brokers

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account email and user ID: retained as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Fitness and health data on your device: retained until you delete the app or clear its data.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then automatically deleted.

6. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Access your data: all your fitness data is visible inside the app.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored fitness data. To delete your account (email and user ID), contact us at abdeyamine@gmail.com.
  • Export your data: request a copy by emailing abdeyamine@gmail.com.
  • Opt out of crash reporting: unfortunately Crashlytics is enabled by default and cannot be disabled in the app yet. You can opt out by uninstalling the app. We plan to add an in-app toggle in a future version.
If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we have, request deletion, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

7. Children's privacy

Bodymodo is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data security

We rely on Google Firebase for authentication and crash reporting, which uses industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, encryption at rest). Your fitness data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox — no other app can access it.

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. International data transfers

Firebase services are operated by Google LLC, headquartered in the United States. If you access Bodymodo from outside the US, your account information may be transferred to and processed in the US. Google maintains appropriate safeguards for international transfers.
