Delete Your Bodymodo Data

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Bodymodo is provided by the Developer Team. This page explains how Bodymodo users can delete data without necessarily deleting their Bodymodo account.

Do not send your password. To process a data deletion request, the Developer Team only needs the email address used for the Bodymodo account, if any, and a short description of the data you want deleted.

Delete local app data without deleting your account

  1. Open the Bodymodo Android app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete local data.
  4. Confirm Delete data.

This removes Bodymodo data stored on that device, including local profile, workout, meal, measurement, reminder, and progress-photo records. If you are signed in, this does not delete your Firebase Authentication account.

Request data deletion outside the app

If you cannot access Bodymodo or want help deleting data that may have been processed by Bodymodo service providers, you can request data deletion by email.

Request data deletion by email

  1. Use the button above, or email the Developer Team using the contact email shown on the Bodymodo Google Play store listing.
  2. Use the subject line Bodymodo data deletion request.
  3. Include the email address used for your Bodymodo account, if you have one.
  4. Describe whether you want local data instructions, support/feedback messages deleted, analytics/crash/AI-related data reviewed, or all deletable data removed while keeping your account.
  5. The Developer Team may ask you to confirm ownership of the account or request before deletion is completed.

The Developer Team will respond to valid deletion requests within 30 days unless a different period is required or allowed by law. Deletion from service-provider backups or logs may take up to 90 days.

Data deleted

  • In-app local data deletion: local Bodymodo profile, workouts, programs, challenges, active workouts, custom workouts, meals, water logs, nutrition settings, measurements, weight logs, reminders, progress-photo records, consent history, and privacy-control settings stored by Bodymodo on that device.
  • Email deletion request: support or feedback messages, account-associated records, optional analytics identifiers/events, crash diagnostics, and AI workout-generation records that can be located, are associated with your request, and can be deleted by the Developer Team or applicable service providers.

Data kept or not controlled remotely

  • Your Firebase Authentication account, account email address, Firebase user ID, and sign-in provider identifiers may be kept if you ask to delete data without deleting your account.
  • Local data stored only on a device cannot be deleted remotely by the Developer Team. To remove local-only data on each device, use the in-app deletion steps above, clear Bodymodo app data from Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Aggregated, de-identified, or non-account-linked analytics and crash diagnostics may be retained when they cannot reasonably be linked back to your request.
  • Support request records, security logs, fraud-prevention records, and legal records may be kept only for as long as needed for support, security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Delete your account instead

If you want to delete your Bodymodo account and associated account data, use the Bodymodo account deletion page.
