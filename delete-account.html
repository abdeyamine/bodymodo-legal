Delete Your Bodymodo Account and Data

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Bodymodo is provided by the Developer Team. This page explains how Bodymodo users can delete an account and request deletion of associated account data.

Do not send your password. To process a deletion request, the Developer Team only needs the email address used for the Bodymodo account and a short message requesting account deletion.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Bodymodo Android app.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete, if you are not already signed in.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Confirm Delete account.

Firebase may require a recent sign-in before the account can be deleted. If that happens, sign in again and repeat the deletion steps.

Request deletion outside the app

If you cannot access Bodymodo or no longer have the app installed, you can request account deletion by email.

Request account deletion by email

  1. Use the button above, or email the Developer Team using the contact email shown on the Bodymodo Google Play store listing.
  2. Use the subject line Bodymodo account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address used for your Bodymodo account.
  4. The Developer Team may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before deletion is completed.

The Developer Team will respond to valid deletion requests within 30 days unless a different period is required or allowed by law. Deletion from service-provider backups or logs may take up to 90 days.

Data deleted

  • Your Bodymodo Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your account email address, Firebase user ID, and sign-in provider identifiers associated with the Bodymodo account, subject to Firebase and legal retention rules.
  • Local Bodymodo data on the device where in-app account deletion is performed, including profile, workout, meal, measurement, reminder, and progress-photo data.

Data kept or not controlled remotely

  • Local data stored only on a device cannot be deleted remotely by the Developer Team if you request deletion outside the app. To remove local-only data, clear Bodymodo app data from Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Aggregated, de-identified, or non-account-linked analytics and crash diagnostics may be retained according to Firebase and Google Analytics settings.
  • Support or feedback emails may be retained as needed for support, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.
  • Records required by law or needed to protect rights and safety may be kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.
